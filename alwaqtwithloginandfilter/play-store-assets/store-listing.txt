Al Waqt is a premium, community-driven, and completely ad-free application designed to help you find local masjids (mosques) and keep track of accurate, up-to-date Jamat and prayer timings.

Whether you are traveling to a new city or looking for Jamat timings in your local neighborhood, Al Waqt provides quick, location-based access to the closest places of worship.

Key Features:
• Accurate Jamat & Prayer Timings: View real-time timings for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Jummah, and Taraweeh.
• Nearby Mosque Finder: Automatically locate masjids near your exact location with built-in map navigation.
• Follow Your Masjids: Save your favorite masjids to quickly access their schedules and view local updates.
• Community Verifications: Participate in keeping timings accurate! Users and Muazzins can suggest updates, upload timing charts, and verify mosque details.
• Events & Announcements: Stay informed about weekly bayans, special lectures, and community events happening at followed masjids.
• Clean & Ad-Free: A completely distraction-free experience designed with beautiful modern aesthetics.

Keep your prayers on time, wherever you are, with Al Waqt.
