Pricing & Refund Policy

100% FREE APP

Al Waqt is entirely free for the community.

1. Pricing Policy

Al Waqt was built as a community service to help Muslims find local mosques and keep track of accurate prayer and Jamat timings.

  • No Purchase Required: There are no premium features, no subscriptions, and no paid options.
  • No Hidden Costs: Downloading and utilizing all the features inside the App (adding a masjid, following a masjid, reporting updates, viewing events) is 100% free.

2. Refund & Cancellation Policy

Since Al Waqt does not charge users for any of its services and does not process any financial transactions, payments, or in-app purchases:

  • No Refunds: There are no refund policies, as no payments are ever collected.
  • No Cancellations: You do not need to cancel any subscription. You can simply uninstall the App from your device at any time if you no longer wish to use it.
