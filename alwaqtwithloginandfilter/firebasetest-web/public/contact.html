Contact & Support

Have questions, encountered a bug, or need help with the Al Waqt app? We are here to support you. Please check the information below on how to reach out to us.

1. Support Contact Details

For all support requests, feature suggestions, or general inquiries, you can reach out directly via email:

📧 Email Support

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 to 48 hours.

2. Requesting Data Deletion

Under our Privacy Policy, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal information.

To request data deletion, please email us at aiyaaz@froarit.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". In the email body, please include:

  • Your full name.
  • The email address associated with your Al Waqt account.

Once we receive your request, we will delete your account and personal details from our active servers within 7 business days and send you a confirmation email.

3. Reporting Incorrect Mosque Details

Al Waqt is a community-driven project. If you notice incorrect Jamat timings, a missing mosque, or a mosque that has closed down:

  • Use the built-in "Report" or "Verify" buttons directly on the Mosque Details card inside the App.
  • Alternatively, you can email us with the mosque name, city, and correct details/photos, and we will update the database manually.
